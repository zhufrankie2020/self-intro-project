## 1. Purpose

Reframe the existing self-introduction website as a timeless, public professional profile for Frankie (Yifan) Zhu.

The profile should establish Frankie as a **Global Transformation & Technology Leader** with strength in strategic thinking, transformation delivery, technology-enabled change and people leadership. It should also reveal his personality through real hobby stories that create an “aha” moment for the reader.

The current employer must not be named. Employer-specific programme information and internal delivery details must not appear on the public site.

## 2. Audience and intended outcome

Primary audience:

- senior leaders and hiring decision-makers;
- professional peers and potential collaborators;
- recruiters and people exploring Frankie's experience;
- colleagues who want to understand how Frankie thinks and leads.

The reader should leave with three impressions:

1. Frankie can turn complex business challenges into clear transformation direction.
2. He can translate strategy into practical delivery, governance and measurable outcomes.
3. He is an experienced, thoughtful and human leader with interests beyond work.

## 9. Accessibility, responsive design and graceful failure

- Preserve keyboard navigation for the command menu and interactive controls.
- Add meaningful alternative text for the avatar.
- Ensure links and buttons have clear focus states.
- Preserve readable colour contrast on the dark background.
- Avoid placing important text only inside hover states or tooltips.
- Stack timeline items, tabs and hobby cards cleanly on narrow screens.
- If an interaction fails, keep the underlying story and facts visible.
- External links should remain normal clickable links even if enhanced JavaScript fails.
